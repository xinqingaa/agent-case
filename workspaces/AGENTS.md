# Workspace Rules

- Read `project.yaml` before any project-specific investigation.
- Do not exceed a false, absent, or unspecified permission capability.
- Ask the user in plain language for read, run, and network permission; translate the answer into `project.yaml`.
- Record evidence in `EVIDENCE.md` while investigating; do not reconstruct citations after drafting.
- Keep temporary observations under `working/` and reader-facing facts under `detailed/`.
- Do not mark G6 complete while a blocking acceptance item remains.
